# Anniversary Animation

This folder contains a special anniversary animation for you and your partner!

## How to Open
1.  Navigate to the `anniversary` folder.
2.  Double-click on `index.html` to open it in your browser.

## Logic Flow
1.  **Start**: Click "Open Me".
2.  **Display**: Your photos appear separately for a moment.
3.  **Fusion Spin**: The photos will spin around each other like a "Polymerization" effect, trailing hearts.
4.  **Explosion**: A bright flash of light.
5.  **Reveal**: The final combined photo (`bareng.jpeg`) appears with your text.

## Customization
-   **Photos**: Replace images in `assets/` with same names (`afif.jpeg`, `uti.jpeg`, `bareng.jpeg`).
-   **Text**: Edit `index.html` to change the message.
-   **Speed**: Adjust timings in `script.js`.

Enjoy! ❤️
